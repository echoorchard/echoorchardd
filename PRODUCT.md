## Platform

android

## Users

Casual mobile players aged 13+ who want a calm, skill-based game for short
sessions (2–5 minutes): a commute, a queue, a wind-down before sleep. They
like rhythm and timing but do not read music and do not want arcade chaos.
*(inferred)*

## Product Purpose

Echo Orchard is an offline rhythm-timing game. Each tree in an orchard row
pulses on its own tempo; the player taps a tree when its echo ring meets the
golden ripe band to harvest fruit into a basket. Success: a player learns to
"listen with their eyes", clears the five seasons, and keeps coming back for
the daily row and the Long Row.

## Positioning

Rhythm through sight, not sheet music: every beat is a visible, expanding ring
on a living tree, and later seasons layer one new rule at a time (many tempos,
rest beats, wind, fading echoes). Gentle failure (bruised fruit, never
punishment), no network, no accounts.

## Operating Context

Portrait phone, one thumb, often with sound off. Sessions are short and
interrupted, so pause/resume and lifecycle handling matter. Many players play
muted: the visual cue must always be sufficient on its own.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Flutter + Flame, Android only for release; web build used for previews.
- Fully offline: no INTERNET permission, no analytics, no ads, no IAP, no
  accounts, no data collection. Progress is local (`shared_preferences`).
- All art and sound are generated by scripts in `tool/` that ship in the repo.
- Terminology: rows (levels), seasons (chapters), basket, quota, ripe band,
  echo ring, bruised fruit, seeds (earned currency), dewdrops (earned hints).
- Publisher: MUMMY FAITHIA BUSINESS VENTURE, Ilesha, Osun State, Nigeria
  (store listing + website privacy policy only); support comfort@echobelogame.com.ng.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Echo Orchard**. Tagline: "Answer the pulse."
- Voice: warm, unhurried orchard language; never generic "Play / Settings /
  Game Over / Level Select".
- Font: Fraunces (bundled, SIL OFL).

## Evidence on Hand

No testimonials, ratings, press or download numbers exist. Never invent them
in store copy.

## Product Principles

1. The visual beat is always enough: sound and haptics only reinforce.
2. One new rule per season, taught before it is tested.
3. Failure is gentle and fast to retry; restart is always free.
4. Rewards are earned by play only; nothing is for sale.
5. Calm over clutter: one focal thing per screen.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Never colour alone: rest beats, perfect/good/bruise and locked states also
  differ in shape or label.
- Eyes-Only mode (no audio reliance), Gentle Wind, Reduce Motion, text scale
  up to 1.3×, 48dp touch targets, Semantics labels on every control.
