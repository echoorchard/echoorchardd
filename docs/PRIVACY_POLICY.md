# Privacy Policy

**Ripe Rhythm**

**Effective date:** 12 November 2026
**Last updated:** 12 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **MUMMY FAITHIA BUSINESS VENTURE** ("we", "us", "our") handles information in the mobile game **Ripe Rhythm** (the "App"), available on Google Play, and on our website https://echobelogame.com.ng. The App is developed by **Comfort Bukunmi Bello** for MUMMY FAITHIA BUSINESS VENTURE, the developer named on the App's Google Play store listing.

By installing or using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

---

## 1. Summary

- **We don't collect any data.** Nothing about you or how you play is collected, stored on a server, sold or shared.
- **No account or sign-in is needed.** You simply open the App and play.
- The App has **no analytics or tracking** of any kind.
- The App **works fully offline**. It has no network access and does not send any data over the internet.
- The App **requests no device permissions** (no location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files, phone state or internet).
- Your game progress is stored **only on your own device**, and you can delete it at any time.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

**We do not collect any personal or sensitive user data.** That includes:

- Names, email addresses, phone numbers or other contact details
- Location (precise or approximate)
- Device identifiers, tracking identifiers or IP addresses
- Photos, videos, audio, files, contacts or calendar data
- Usage analytics, crash reports or diagnostics
- Financial or payment information
- Health, fitness or any other sensitive information

The App has no server and no network access, so no information from the App ever reaches us.

---

## 3. Information Stored on Your Device

To let you continue where you left off, the App saves a small amount of **game data locally on your device**, in the App's private Android app storage (using the `shared_preferences` library). This data:

- includes your progress (rows cleared, stars earned per row, the furthest season reached), your settings (sound, music, vibration, pulse tick, Eyes-Only rhythm, Gentle Wind and Reduce Motion), your lifetime game stats (fruit harvested, perfect and good harvests, bruised fruit, rows played and won, best streak, Long Row best score, the dates you played and cleared the Daily Row), and your in-game seeds, dewdrops, achievements ("seals") and the baskets and keepsakes you have traded seeds for
- contains no personal information
- is never transmitted to us or to anyone else
- is not accessible to us in any way

Seeds and dewdrops are earned only by playing and have no value outside the App.

---

## 4. How We Use Information

Because we do not collect any information, we do not use, analyse, profile or process any personal data. The game data described in Section 3 is used only by the App itself, on your device, to run the game.

---

## 5. Sharing and Disclosure

**We do not share, sell, rent or trade any user data with any third party**, because we do not collect any. There is no data to share with analytics providers, data brokers or any other companies.

---

## 6. Third-Party Services and SDKs

The App contains **no analytics, crash-reporting, social media or tracking SDKs**.

The App is built with the following open-source software components. None of them collects or transmits any data in this App, and the App has no network access:

- **Flutter** (Google), the app framework
- **Flame**, the game engine that draws and animates the orchard
- **shared_preferences**, which saves your game progress on your device
- **audioplayers**, which plays the game's built-in sound effects and music from files included in the App

The App is distributed through **Google Play**. When you download the App, Google may process information under its own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). We do not receive any personal information about you from Google Play.

## 7. Device Permissions

The App **does not request any Android permissions**. Its release manifest requests no permissions at all, not even INTERNET. It cannot access your location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage, photos, phone calls or messages, and it does not need or use internet access.

Our website, https://echobelogame.com.ng, has no analytics, tracking cookies or forms. ## 10. Data Security

Because no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is no personal data for us to store or protect on any server. Your local game data stays inside the App's private storage on your device, which Android protects from other apps. Support emails are kept in a password-protected mailbox that only we can access.

---

## 11. Data Retention and Deletion

- **We retain no user data from the App**, because it collects none.
- Your local game data stays on your device until you delete it.
- **To delete all game data**, you can:
  1. open the App and go to **Wind & Hush → Reset the orchard**, or
  2. uninstall the App, or
  3. clear the App's storage in your device settings (**Settings → Apps → Ripe Rhythm → Storage → Clear data**).

The App has no accounts, so there is no account to delete. Deleted game data cannot be recovered. To have a support email deleted sooner, just ask us.

---

## 12. Children's Privacy

Ripe Rhythm is a calm, non-violent rhythm game for a general audience aged 13 and over. **It is not directed at children under 13**, and **we do not collect any personal information from anyone, including children** (under 13, or the equivalent minimum age in your country). The App has no accounts, no chat, no user-generated content, and no way to contact other players.

If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, please contact us and we will delete it.
